PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION · 2026

11 OCTOBER 2026 SHRINGRI HIMAL ICE–ROCK AVALANCHE AND FLASH-FLOOD EVENT

Budi Gandaki River Corridor | Gorkha, Nepal

A preliminary investigation of the October 2026 event using satellite imagery. The study examines changes along the affected river corridor, the blockage near Shyar Khola, and the potential source area upstream of Udilung Khola. Initial geological interpretation considers the possible involvement of rocks belonging to the Tethyan Sedimentary Sequence.
